# Wheat Farming Guide — India

## Sowing
- Best sowing window: October 25 – November 25 (North India plains)
- Delayed sowing (after Nov 25) reduces yield by 30–35 kg/ha per day of delay
- Seed rate: 100 kg/ha for timely sowing, 125 kg/ha for late sowing
- Recommended varieties: HD 2967, HD 3086, GW 496, DBW 187, PBW 723

## Fertilizer (NPK)
- Timely sown: 120:60:40 kg NPK/ha
- Late sown: 90:60:40 kg NPK/ha
- Apply full P and K at sowing; split N into 3 doses — 50% at sowing, 25% at first irrigation (CRI stage, 20–21 days), 25% at second irrigation
- Use urea for nitrogen, DAP or SSP for phosphorus, MOP for potassium

## Irrigation
- 4–6 irrigations required depending on soil type
- Critical stages: CRI (20–21 DAS), tillering (40–45 DAS), jointing (60–65 DAS), flowering (80–85 DAS), grain filling (100–105 DAS)
- Avoid waterlogging — wheat is sensitive to standing water

## Major Diseases
### Yellow Rust (Stripe Rust)
- Yellow stripes of powder on leaves, parallel to the leaf veins
- Favoured by cool, moist weather (10–15°C)
- Control: Propiconazole 25EC @ 0.1% or Tebuconazole 250 EW @ 0.1% spray; use resistant varieties (HD 3086, PBW 752)

### Brown Rust (Leaf Rust)
- Circular orange-brown pustules scattered on leaves
- Spreads rapidly in warm weather (15–25°C)
- Control: Mancozeb 75WP @ 2g/L or Propiconazole 25EC @ 1ml/L

### Loose Smut
- Grains replaced by black powdery spore mass; head turns black before flowering
- Seed-borne disease — treat seed with Carboxin 37.5% + Thiram 37.5% @ 3g/kg seed before sowing

### Karnal Bunt
- Partial conversion of grain to black smelly powder
- Spray Propiconazole 25EC at boot leaf stage and again 10 days later

## Major Pests
### Aphids
- Cluster on leaves and ears; suck sap; cause yellowing
- Peak damage: February–March
- Control: Imidacloprid 17.8SL @ 0.5ml/L or Thiamethoxam 25WG @ 0.5g/L; natural predators like ladybird beetles help

### Termites
- Damage roots; entire plants dry up (dead heart)
- Control: Chlorpyrifos 20EC @ 4L/ha mixed in irrigation water or soil drench at sowing

## Harvest
- Harvest when grain moisture is 12–14%
- Avoid delayed harvest — grain shattering and quality loss
- Store at <12% moisture with proper aeration

## Yield Targets
- Irrigated timely sown: 50–55 q/ha
- Late sown: 35–40 q/ha
