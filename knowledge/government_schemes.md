# Indian Government Agricultural Schemes — Complete Guide

## PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

### What is it?
Direct income support of ₹6,000/year to eligible farmer families in 3 equal installments of ₹2,000 every 4 months.

### Who is eligible?
- All land-holding farmer families with cultivable land
- Both small, marginal and large farmers

### Who is NOT eligible?
- Institutional landholders
- Farmers who are or were income tax payers
- Constitutional post holders, current/former ministers, MPs, MLAs, mayors
- Government employees (except Group D / multi-tasking staff)

### How to register?
1. Visit pmkisan.gov.in
2. Go to "Farmers Corner" → "New Farmer Registration"
3. Required: Aadhaar number, bank account number, mobile number, land records
4. Can also register at Common Service Centres (CSC) or local patwari/agriculture office

### Check status
- pmkisan.gov.in → "Farmers Corner" → "Beneficiary Status"
- PM-KISAN helpline: 155261 / 1800-115-526 (toll-free)

## PMFBY — Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (Crop Insurance)

### What is it?
Crop insurance scheme covering losses from natural calamities, pests, diseases.

### Premium rates (Farmer's share)
- Kharif crops: 2% of sum insured
- Rabi crops: 1.5% of sum insured
- Horticulture/commercial crops: 5% of sum insured
- Government pays the balance premium

### What is covered?
- Prevented sowing (due to drought/flood)
- Standing crop loss (all stages)
- Post-harvest losses (for 14 days after harvest)
- Localized calamities (hailstorm, landslide, inundation)

### How to enroll?
- Loanee farmers: automatically enrolled via KCC; can opt out
- Non-loanee farmers: apply through bank, CSC, or PMFBY portal (pmfby.gov.in)
- **Deadline: 2 weeks before end of sowing period** — do not miss

### How to claim?
- Notify within 72 hours of crop damage via: Crop Insurance App, 14447 helpline, or nearest bank/agriculture office
- Take photos/video of damage as evidence

## Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

### What is it?
Revolving credit facility for farmers at subsidized interest rate.

### Interest rate
- Effective rate: ~4% per year (after 3% interest subvention from govt for prompt repayment)
- Normal rate without subvention: 7% per year

### What can you use it for?
- Crop production expenses (seeds, fertilizers, pesticides)
- Post-harvest expenses
- Farm maintenance
- Allied activities: fisheries, animal husbandry

### Loan limit
- Based on land holding, crop, and scale of finance
- Short-term limit for crops + 10% for post-harvest expenses + 20% for maintenance

### How to apply?
- Visit nearest bank (SBI, cooperative bank, regional rural bank)
- Required: land records, identity proof, Aadhaar, passport photo
- Simplified application form available at all banks

## Soil Health Card Scheme

### What is it?
Free soil testing every 2 years; government issues a card showing soil nutrient status and fertilizer recommendations.

### How to get it?
- Contact local agriculture department or KVK
- Give a soil sample from your field (instructions provided)
- Card issued in 6–8 weeks with crop-specific fertilizer recommendations

### Portal: soilhealth.dac.gov.in
- Check your card status online
- Download previous cards

## PMKSY — PM Krishi Sinchai Yojana

### What is it?
Subsidy on micro-irrigation (drip and sprinkler systems) to promote water use efficiency.

### Subsidy
- Small/Marginal farmers (up to 2 ha): 55% subsidy on drip; 55% on sprinkler
- Other farmers: 45% on drip; 45% on sprinkler

### How to apply?
- Apply through state horticulture or agriculture department
- Scheme name may vary by state (e.g., "Micro-Irrigation Scheme" in Maharashtra)
- Contact block-level agriculture officer

## eNAM — National Agriculture Market

### What is it?
Online trading platform connecting farmers with buyers across India for better price discovery.

### Benefits
- Transparent auction — price visible to all
- Access to buyers beyond local mandi
- Payment directly to bank account
- Quality assaying services available

### How to use?
- Visit enam.gov.in or download eNAM app
- Register as farmer with Aadhaar and bank details
- Available at 1,000+ registered mandis across India
- Speak to mandi secretary for assistance

---

## RKVY — Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana

### What is it?
Flexible state-level scheme for farm infrastructure and development.

### What's covered?
- Farm mechanization
- Post-harvest infrastructure
- Horticulture development
- Agricultural marketing

### How to access?
- Contact district agriculture officer or state agriculture department
- Funds allocated to states — apply at district level

---

## PM Kisan Maan Dhan Yojana (Pension Scheme)

### What is it?
Pension scheme for small and marginal farmers (up to 2 ha land).

### Benefit
₹3,000/month pension after age 60.

### How to join?
- Age 18–40 years; contribute ₹55–₹200/month (based on age)
- Government matches contribution
- Enroll at CSC with Aadhaar and bank details

---

## NFSM — National Food Security Mission

### What is it?
Mission to increase production of rice, wheat, pulses, coarse cereals, and commercial crops.

### Benefits to farmers
- Subsidized certified seeds of improved varieties
- Demonstration plots at KVKs
- Farm machinery on subsidy
- Crop management inputs

### How to access?
- Contact block agriculture office or KVK for current season's programme
