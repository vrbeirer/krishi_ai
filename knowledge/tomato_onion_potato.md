# Vegetable Crops Guide — Tomato, Onion, Potato (India)

---

## TOMATO

### Nursery and Transplanting
- Sow nursery 25–30 days before transplanting
- Seed rate: 400–500g/ha; treat with Thiram 2g/kg seed
- Transplant at 4–5 leaf stage; spacing 60x45 cm
- Varieties: Pusa Ruby, Pusa 120, Arka Vikas, hybrid varieties for market

### Fertilizer
- 120:80:80 kg NPK/ha
- Apply FYM 25 tonnes/ha before transplanting
- Split N in 3 doses — at transplanting, 30 days after, at fruit set

### Major Diseases
#### Early Blight (Alternaria solani)
- Dark brown concentric ring spots on lower leaves; yellow halo
- Control: Mancozeb 75WP @ 2g/L or Chlorothalonil 75WP @ 2g/L every 7–10 days

#### Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans)
- Water-soaked dark brown lesions on leaves and fruits; white mould on underside
- Spreads rapidly in cool, wet weather (15–20°C)
- Control: Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.5g/L; act immediately — disease spreads very fast

#### Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (ToLCV)
- Leaf curling upward, yellowing, stunted growth; transmitted by whitefly
- No cure — control whitefly with Imidacloprid 0.3ml/L; remove infected plants

#### Fusarium/Verticillium Wilt
- Yellowing of lower leaves; brown discolouration in stem cross-section
- Soil treatment with Trichoderma viride @ 2.5 kg/ha; use resistant varieties

#### Damping Off (Nursery)
- Seedlings collapse at soil level in nursery
- Drench nursery soil with Copper oxychloride 3g/L or Carbendazim 1g/L

### Major Pests
#### Fruit Borer (Helicoverpa armigera)
- Larvae bore into green fruits; entry hole visible
- Control: Spinosad 45SC @ 0.3ml/L or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5SC @ 0.3ml/L

#### Whitefly
- Leaf curl virus vector; sticky honeydew and sooty mould
- Control: Thiamethoxam 25WG @ 0.2g/L or Flonicamid 50WG @ 0.3g/L

#### Mites (Red Spider Mite)
- Bronzing, stippling on leaves; fine webbing visible
- Control: Abamectin 1.9EC @ 0.5ml/L or Spiromesifen 22.9SC @ 1ml/L

## ONION

### Nursery and Transplanting
- Kharif: sow nursery June–July; transplant August–September
- Rabi: sow nursery October–November; transplant December–January
- Seed rate: 8–10 kg/ha; nursery area 0.05 ha for 1 ha main field
- Spacing: 15x10 cm; transplant 6–8 week old seedlings

### Fertilizer
- 100:50:50 kg NPK/ha
- Split N in 3 doses — at transplanting, 30 and 60 days after
- Sulphur @ 30 kg/ha improves bulb size and pungency (use SSP or gypsum)

### Major Diseases
#### Purple Blotch (Alternaria porri)
- Water-soaked lesions with purple centre and yellow margin on leaves
- Control: Iprodione 50WP @ 2g/L or Mancozeb 75WP @ 2.5g/L

#### Stemphylium Blight
- Small, water-soaked spots turning yellow; leaf tip dieback
- Control: Copper oxychloride 50WP @ 3g/L

#### Downy Mildew (Peronospora destructor)
- Pale green to yellow patches on leaves with violet-grey mould
- Control: Metalaxyl + Mancozeb @ 2.5g/L; avoid overhead irrigation

#### Basal Rot (Fusarium oxysporum)
- Rotting of basal plate; roots die; yellowing and plant death
- Use healthy bulbs; soil drench with Carbendazim 1g/L

### Major Pests
#### Thrips (Thrips tabaci)
- Silvery-white streaks on leaves; severe infestation causes leaf drying
- Most damaging pest of onion
- Control: Fipronil 5SC @ 1.5ml/L or Spinosad 45SC @ 0.3ml/L; use blue sticky traps

### Harvesting
- Harvest when 50% tops fall over naturally
- Cure in field for 7–10 days; dry in shade for storage
- Storage onions should have dry outer scales; moisture causes rotting

## POTATO

### Planting
- North India plains: October 15 – November 15 (main season)
- Hills: March–April
- Seed rate: 2–2.5 tonnes/ha (cut tubers 25–40g each with 2–3 eyes)
- Treat seed with Carbendazim 2g/kg or Mancozeb 3g/kg before cutting
- Spacing: 60x20 cm

### Fertilizer
- 180:80:100 kg NPK/ha; apply in 2 splits (at planting and earthing up)
- Potash is critical for tuber quality and starch content
- FYM 25 tonnes/ha before planting

### Major Diseases
#### Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans) — Most Destructive
- Water-soaked dark lesions on leaves; white mould on underside; tuber rot
- Spreads explosively in cool, humid conditions (10–20°C)
- Preventive spray: Mancozeb 75WP @ 2g/L from 30 DAS every 7 days
- Curative: Metalaxyl + Mancozeb @ 2.5g/L; act within 24 hours of first symptoms
- Never use water from an infected field for irrigation

#### Early Blight (Alternaria solani)
- Brown concentric ring spots on older leaves
- Control: Mancozeb 75WP @ 2.5g/L

#### Common Scab (Streptomyces scabies)
- Corky scab lesions on tuber surface; quality loss
- Maintain soil pH 5.2–5.5; avoid liming before potato

#### Blackleg and Soft Rot (Pectobacterium)
- Blackening of stem base; slimy rotting of tubers in wet soil
- Use certified seed; avoid waterlogging

### Major Pests
#### Whitefly
- Transmits potato leaf curl virus; control with Imidacloprid 0.3ml/L

#### Aphids (Myzus persicae)
- Transmit viruses; use virus-free certified seed for next season
- Control: Thiamethoxam 25WG @ 0.2g/L

#### Potato Tuber Moth
- Larvae mine leaves and bore into tubers in storage
- Earthing up covers tubers; use Spinosad 45SC @ 0.4ml/L

### Harvest and Storage
- Harvest when tops die naturally (70–90 DAS for early varieties)
- Avoid harvesting in wet conditions — tubers bruise and rot
- Store at 2–4°C for long-term; 10–15°C for seed potatoes
- Cure at 15–20°C for 10–15 days before cold storage

## Yield Targets
- Tomato: 250–350 q/ha (hybrid); 150–200 q/ha (open pollinated)
- Onion: 250–300 q/ha (kharif lower due to pink root and thrips)
- Potato: 250–350 q/ha (plains irrigated)
