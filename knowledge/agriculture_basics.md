# Krishi.ai reference notes: safe general agricultural guidance

These notes provide general process guidance, not a substitute for a crop- and district-specific recommendation. When a question asks for a precise fertilizer rate, pesticide/herbicide product or dose, irrigation schedule, disease diagnosis, crop choice, or treatment, first collect the relevant details and use a current, region-appropriate reference.

## Soil testing and fertilizer decisions

Avoid giving a single fertilizer mixture or rate as suitable for every crop and field. Nutrient requirements depend on crop, soil conditions, target yield, growth stage, and local recommendations. Encourage the farmer to use their Soil Health Card or arrange a soil test and follow the fertilizer/advisory recommendations associated with that field. The Government of India's Soil Health Card information describes soil status using nutrient and soil parameters including N, P, K, S, micronutrients, pH, electrical conductivity, and organic carbon, and gives fertilizer/soil-amendment recommendations based on the tested soil.

Official reference: Government of India, Soil Health Card portal and FAQs: https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in/ and https://support.soilhealth.dac.gov.in/kb/?lang=en-US

## Pest and disease management

Do not diagnose a pest or disease with certainty from a short text description alone. Ask for crop, variety if known, growth stage, district, symptoms, how much of the field is affected, and any recent sprays or fertilizer applications. A clear photo of affected leaves, stems, fruit, and the whole plant can help with triage but does not replace local confirmation where stakes are high.

Prefer integrated pest management (IPM): monitor the field, identify the pest correctly, and consider suitable cultural, mechanical, biological, and other non-chemical options. Do not jump straight to a chemical recommendation based on one symptom. If a pesticide is being considered, tell the farmer to verify that the product is currently registered and labelled for the exact crop and pest, follow the product label and local agricultural guidance, use required protective equipment, and protect pollinators, people, animals, and water. Do not invent or guess mixing rates, application intervals, tank mixes, or waiting periods.

Official reference: Directorate of Plant Protection, Quarantine & Storage (Government of India), Integrated Pest Management training material: https://ppqs.gov.in/

## Weather and farm operations

Weather-related farm decisions should use a current, location-specific forecast or agricultural advisory. Ask for the state, district and preferably block/tehsil, plus the crop and growth stage. Do not invent current rainfall probability, temperature, wind speed, warnings, or spray windows. Krishi.ai may include a current/three-day Open-Meteo model forecast when a location is explicitly supplied. Distinguish that model forecast from an official IMD warning and direct the farmer to the India Meteorological Department's current district/block forecasts and agricultural advisories for operational decisions.

Official reference: India Meteorological Department, Agricultural Meteorology Services and Agromet Advisories: https://mausam.imd.gov.in/imd_latest/contents/meteorological-agriculture-services.php and https://mausam.imd.gov.in/responsive/agromet_adv_ser_block_current_en.php

## Market prices

Do not claim a current mandi price unless a live, dated, location-specific market data source is actually available. Krishi.ai can query the Government of India data.gov.in mandi dataset only when its API key and resource ID are configured and the farmer gives a supported commodity and explicit location. Ask for the crop/variety, state, mandi and date. Make clear whether a price is reported, estimated, historical, or unavailable; do not recommend a sale solely from an unverified price.

## Minimum details for a useful crop-specific answer

When relevant, ask for: (1) crop and variety, (2) state/district, (3) growth stage or sowing date, (4) symptoms and when they began, (5) soil test or irrigation status, and (6) recent pesticide/fertilizer use. Avoid asking for all details if one specific follow-up is enough to proceed.
