# Oilseed Crops Guide — Soybean, Groundnut, Mustard (India)

---

## SOYBEAN

### Sowing
- Kharif crop; sow after first 50–75mm monsoon rain (June 20 – July 15)
- Seed rate: 75–80 kg/ha (large seed) or 65–70 kg/ha (small seed)
- Spacing: 45x5 cm or 30x10 cm
- Seed treatment: Rhizobium + PSB inoculants + Thiram 2g/kg seed
- Varieties: JS 335, JS 9305, MACS 58, NRC 86, Phule Agrani

### Fertilizer
- 30:60:40 kg NPK/ha (low N because of N-fixation by Rhizobium)
- Apply full dose at sowing
- Sulphur @ 20 kg/ha as gypsum improves protein and oil content

### Major Diseases
#### Yellow Mosaic Virus (YMV)
- Yellow patches and mosaic on leaves; transmitted by whitefly
- No cure; remove and destroy infected plants early
- Control whitefly: Imidacloprid 17.8SL @ 0.5ml/L
- Use resistant varieties: JS 9305, NRC 86

#### Soybean Rust (Phakopsora pachyrhizi)
- Orange-brown pustules on underside of leaves; rapid defoliation
- Control: Tebuconazole 25.9EC @ 1ml/L or Hexaconazole 5SC @ 2ml/L at first sign

#### Charcoal Rot (Macrophomina phaseolina)
- Shredding of stem cortex; black sclerotia inside stem; wilting in hot dry conditions
- Use certified seed; avoid moisture stress at pod fill stage

### Major Pests
#### Stem Fly (Melanagromyza sojae)
- Larvae mine into stem; dead heart at early stage; stem blackens internally
- Most damaging in delayed sowing; Control: Dimethoate 30EC @ 2ml/L at 10 DAS

#### Girdle Beetle (Obereopsis brevis)
- Girdling of stem; plant breaks at girdle point; pods dry out
- Remove and destroy infested plants; Control: Chlorpyrifos 20EC @ 2ml/L

#### Leaf Eating Caterpillar / Spodoptera
- Skeletonizing of leaves; defoliation
- Control: Chlorantraniliprole 18.5SC @ 0.3ml/L or Emamectin benzoate 5SG @ 0.4g/L

### Harvest
- Harvest when 85% pods turn brown (100–110 DAS for most varieties)
- Thresh immediately; avoid field drying which causes pod shattering
- Dry to 12% moisture for safe storage

## GROUNDNUT (PEANUT)

### Sowing
- Kharif: June 15 – July 15; Rabi: November–December (South India)
- Seed rate: 80–100 kg/ha (shelled); treat with Thiram 3g/kg + Carbendazim 1g/kg
- Spacing: 30x10 cm; sow 5–6 cm deep
- Varieties: TAG 24, TG 37A, GG 20, ICGV 91114, Phule Pragati

### Fertilizer
- 20:60:40 kg NPK/ha + Gypsum 250 kg/ha at pegging stage (critical for pod filling)
- Seed inoculation with Rhizobium + PSB
- Calcium from gypsum prevents empty pods (unfilled shells)

### Major Diseases
#### Tikka Disease / Leaf Spot (Cercospora)
- Early leaf spot: brown circular spots with yellow halo on upper leaf surface
- Late leaf spot: dark brown spots on lower surface; severe defoliation
- Control: Carbendazim 50WP @ 1g/L or Chlorothalonil 75WP @ 2g/L at 30, 45, 60 DAS

#### Stem Rot / White Mould (Sclerotium rolfsii)
- Rotting of stem at soil level; white mycelium and mustard-seed-like sclerotia
- Control: Soil drench with Carbendazim; Trichoderma in soil at sowing

#### Aflatoxin (Aspergillus flavus)
- Develops in drought-stressed plants and poorly stored pods
- Avoid moisture stress at pod filling; dry pods quickly to 8% moisture for storage
- Aflatoxin is a serious health hazard — critical for export quality

### Major Pests
#### Thrips and Aphids
- Transmit bud necrosis virus; leaf curl and stunting
- Control: Imidacloprid 17.8SL @ 0.5ml/L; yellow sticky traps

#### White Grub (Root Grub)
- C-shaped grubs eat roots; plant wilts and dies in patches
- Soil treatment: Chlorpyrifos 20EC @ 3L/ha mixed in soil at sowing

## MUSTARD (RAPESEED-MUSTARD)

### Sowing
- Rabi crop; sow October 1–31 (North India); early sowing critical for yield
- Seed rate: 5–6 kg/ha (line sowing); 3–4 kg/ha (dibbling)
- Spacing: 30x10 cm
- Varieties: Pusa Bold, RH 749, RH 30, Varuna, Pusa Mustard 25

### Fertilizer
- 80:40:40 kg NPK/ha + Sulphur 40 kg/ha (sulphur is critical for oil quality)
- Apply full P, K, S at sowing; split N — 50% at sowing, 50% at first irrigation (20–25 DAS)

### Irrigation
- 2–3 irrigations; critical at branching (25–30 DAS) and pod filling (70–75 DAS)
- Avoid waterlogging — causes Sclerotinia wilt

### Major Diseases
#### Alternaria Blight (Alternaria brassicae)
- Concentric ring spots on leaves, siliqua and stem; black spots on seeds
- Most destructive disease; Control: Iprodione 50WP @ 2g/L at flowering

#### White Rust (Albugo candida)
- White blistery pustules on underside of leaves; distortion of stem (staghead)
- Control: Metalaxyl + Mancozeb 72WP @ 2.5g/L

#### Sclerotinia Stem Rot
- Watery stem rot; white cottony growth; black sclerotia inside stem; plant collapses
- Favoured by cool, moist conditions; Control: Carbendazim 50WP @ 1g/L at 50% flowering

### Major Pests
#### Aphids (Lipaphis erysimi) — Most Critical Pest
- Dense colonies on young shoots, flowers and pods; cause shriveled seeds
- Threshold: 26 aphids/plant; Control: Dimethoate 30EC @ 1.5ml/L or Oxydemeton methyl @ 1ml/L
- One timely spray can save 25–30% yield

#### Painted Bug (Bagrada hilaris)
- Black and orange bug; sucks sap from seedlings and young plants
- Control: Malathion 50EC @ 2ml/L; early morning spray when bugs inactive

### Harvest
- Harvest when 75% siliquae turn yellow-brown; early morning to reduce shattering
- Thresh on same day as cutting
- Dry seeds to 8% moisture before storage

## Yield Targets
- Soybean: 18–25 q/ha
- Groundnut: 20–25 q/ha (pods)
- Mustard: 18–22 q/ha
