# Cotton Farming Guide — India

## Sowing
- Kharif crop; sow after first good monsoon rain (June–July)
- Optimal sowing: June 1–30 in central India; June 15 – July 15 in south India
- Seed rate: Bt hybrid 750g–1kg/ha (450g per packet covers 1 acre)
- Spacing: 90x60 cm (irrigated); 90x45 cm (rainfed)
- Popular Bt hybrids: Bunny BG-II, RCH 2 BG-II, MRC 7017 BG-II

## Fertilizer (NPK)
- Irrigated: 150:75:75 kg NPK/ha
- Rainfed: 60:30:30 kg NPK/ha
- Apply full P and K at sowing; split N — 1/3 at sowing, 1/3 at squaring (45–50 DAS), 1/3 at boll formation (70–80 DAS)
- Foliar spray of 2% urea at boll development improves yield

## Irrigation
- Critical stages: squaring, flowering, and boll development
- Avoid water stress at flowering — causes boll shedding
- Stop irrigation 15–20 days before harvest

## Major Pests (Most Critical Crop for Pest Management)
### Bollworm Complex
#### American Bollworm (Helicoverpa armigera)
- Larvae bore into squares, flowers and bolls; entry hole with frass visible
- Bt cotton controls this but resistance is developing
- Control: Chlorantraniliprole 18.5SC @ 0.3ml/L or Spinosad 45SC @ 0.3ml/L

#### Pink Bollworm (Pectinophora gossypiella)
- Larvae bore inside bolls; rosette flower, double seed (hallmark symptom)
- Major Bt resistance problem — do not delay harvest
- Control: Thiodicarb 75WP @ 2g/L; pheromone traps @ 5/ha for monitoring

### Sucking Pests
#### Whitefly (Bemisia tabaci)
- Yellowing, leaf curl (virus vector); honeydew causes sooty mould
- Spray early morning; Control: Flonicamid 50WG @ 0.3g/L or Spiromesifen 22.9SC @ 1ml/L
- Rotate insecticide groups to prevent resistance

#### Mealybug (Phenacoccus solenopsis)
- White cottony masses on stem and leaves; yellowing and wilting
- Control: Profenofos 50EC @ 2ml/L or Buprofezin 25SC @ 2ml/L; remove and destroy affected plants early

#### Thrips
- Silvery streaks on leaves; upward leaf curling
- Control: Spinosad 45SC @ 0.3ml/L or Fipronil 5SC @ 1.5ml/L

#### Aphids and Jassids
- Jassids cause downward leaf curling (hopper burn)
- Control: Imidacloprid 17.8SL @ 0.3ml/L; use hairy-leaf varieties resistant to jassids

### Mirid Bug (Gall Midge on Cotton)
- Damage to terminal buds; multiple branching
- Control: Dimethoate 30EC @ 2ml/L

## Major Diseases
### Root Rot / Seedling Wilt (Rhizoctonia, Fusarium)
- Seedlings collapse at soil level
- Seed treatment: Thiram 75WS @ 3g/kg + Carbendazim 50WP @ 2g/kg

### Grey Mildew (Ramularia areola)
- White powdery growth on lower leaf surface; angular spots
- Control: Difenoconazole 25EC @ 0.5ml/L

### Cotton Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuV)
- Upward or downward leaf curling, thickened veins, enation on underside
- Transmitted by whitefly; no cure once infected
- Prevention: control whitefly strictly; remove infected plants

### Fusarium Wilt
- Yellowing of leaves from base upward; brown discolouration in stem cross-section
- Soil-borne; use resistant varieties; soil drench with Carbendazim

## Harvest
- First picking: 130–150 DAS; pick when bolls open fully
- Pick every 10–15 days; 3–5 pickings per season
- Avoid picking wet cotton; store in dry conditions
- Do not mix Bt and non-Bt seed cotton

## Yield Targets
- Irrigated Bt hybrid: 20–25 q lint/ha
- Rainfed: 8–12 q lint/ha
