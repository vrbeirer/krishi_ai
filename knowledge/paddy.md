# Paddy (Rice) Farming Guide — India

## Nursery and Transplanting
- Sow nursery: June 1–25 (kharif); 1 kg seed produces seedlings for 25–30 sq m nursery
- Seed rate: 20–25 kg/ha for transplanted rice, 80–100 kg/ha for direct seeded
- Transplant at 20–25 days (short duration) or 25–30 days (medium duration)
- Spacing: 20x15 cm or 20x20 cm; 2–3 seedlings per hill
- Recommended varieties: Swarna (MTU 7029), IR 64, Pusa Basmati 1121, BPT 5204, CO 51

## Fertilizer (NPK)
- Short duration (105–115 days): 80:40:40 kg NPK/ha
- Medium duration (125–140 days): 100:50:50 kg NPK/ha
- Apply full P and K at transplanting; split N in 3 parts — basal, tillering (25 DAT), panicle initiation
- Zinc deficiency common in India — apply ZnSO4 @ 25 kg/ha as basal

## Water Management
- Maintain 5 cm standing water for first 3 weeks after transplanting
- Allow field to dry (AWD method) between irrigations to save water and reduce methane
- Drain field 10–15 days before harvest for proper grain filling and easy harvesting

## Major Diseases
### Blast (Pyricularia oryzae)
- Diamond-shaped lesions with grey centre and brown margin on leaves; neck rot causes white/dead panicles
- Spreads in cool, humid conditions (24–28°C, high humidity)
- Control: Tricyclazole 75WP @ 0.6g/L or Isoprothiolane 40EC @ 1.5ml/L; spray at tillering and panicle initiation

### Brown Plant Hopper (BPH)
- Circular patches of dead/burnt crop (hopper burn)
- Sucks phloem sap at base of plant
- Control: Buprofezin 25SC @ 1ml/L or Pymetrozine 50WG @ 0.3g/L; avoid excess nitrogen

### Bacterial Leaf Blight (BLB)
- Yellowing and drying of leaf tips; water-soaked margins turn yellow then white
- Spreads in flood conditions and high humidity
- Control: Copper oxychloride 50WP @ 3g/L; use resistant varieties; drain excess water

### Sheath Blight (Rhizoctonia solani)
- Oval greyish-green lesions on leaf sheath near water line; white mycelium in humid conditions
- Control: Hexaconazole 5SC @ 2ml/L or Propiconazole 25EC @ 1ml/L at early stage

### False Smut
- Individual grains replaced by orange/green powdery balls
- Cosmetic but reduces quality; apply Propiconazole at booting stage

## Major Pests
### Stem Borer (Yellow, White, Pink)
- Dead heart in vegetative stage; white ear (panicle) at reproductive stage
- Control: Chlorantraniliprole 0.4G @ 10kg/ha granules in standing water, or Cartap hydrochloride 4G @ 17 kg/ha

### Leaf Folder
- Leaves rolled and tied; caterpillar feeds inside; typical transparent leaf streaks
- Control: Chlorpyrifos 20EC @ 2.5ml/L or Emamectin benzoate 5SG @ 0.4g/L

### Gall Midge
- Dead central shoot (silver shoot) at tillering; no grain formation
- Control: Carbofuran 3G @ 17 kg/ha at early tillering; use resistant varieties

## Harvest
- Harvest at 80–85% grain maturity (20–22% moisture)
- Delay causes shattering; early harvest affects milling quality
- Dry grain to 14% moisture before storage

## Yield Targets
- Irrigated high-yielding varieties: 55–65 q/ha
- Rainfed lowland: 25–35 q/ha
